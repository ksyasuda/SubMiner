- The media timing review controller layout can now be changed in the new **Review media timing** group of `Alt+C`. Learn records a single button or a combo (hold one button, press another), any button can be a hold button, changes are saved per controller, rows warn about clashing inputs, and **Reset all** restores the default layout. The review's button hints follow your bindings.
- A newly opened `Alt+C` window shows and edits your saved controller's bindings until it sees the controller (browsers hide a pad from a new window until a button is pressed in it); before, it showed and edited the global defaults, and the status could stay stuck on "No controllers detected".
