- New controller actions for subtitle lines, in the **Subtitles** group of `Alt+C`: **Previous Subtitle**, **Next Subtitle**, **Replay Subtitle** and **Play Next Subtitle**, matching `Shift+H`, `Shift+L`, `Ctrl+Shift+H` and `Ctrl+Shift+L`. They are unbound by default and work with or without keyboard-only mode.
